When production grows, a notebook is no longer enough

Making custom furniture means dozens of parallel orders, each at its own stage. A kitchen is waiting for edge banding, a built-in wardrobe is at measuring, a reception desk is heading to installation. When all of that is tracked in your head, a notebook or Excel, it is easy to miss a deadline, forget a material or not know the exact status when a customer calls.

Datamaks builds custom software specifically for furniture manufacturers. You do not adapt to an off-the-shelf program. The system follows your way of working, from taking the order to delivery and installation.

What the software tracks

Everything in one place, available to the whole team, on the shop floor and in the office.

Orders by stage

Every order through the production stages: drawing and measurements, cutting, CNC, edge banding, installation, finishing, ready. You see right away where everything is.

Materials and stock

Material used per order and stock levels, with an alert when particleboard, edge banding or hardware fall below the minimum.

Deadlines by stage

A deadline for every order and stage, with a reminder when delivery is near. Fewer delays and less tension with customers.

Reports

Load by stage, number of active orders and urgent deadlines. A clear picture for planning and decisions.

Attachments to orders

Drawings, measurements and photos attached directly to the order, available to the craftsman on the shop floor.

Customers and history

Customer details, previous orders and what was agreed, all in one place when you need it.

Try the live prototype

Open the prototype and see right away what production tracking looks like: a board by stage, materials, deadlines and finances, all filled in with examples. If you like, add your own order and follow it. It runs in the browser, with no installation.

Frequently asked questions

What does furniture production tracking software do?

It tracks every order through the production stages (drawing and measurements, cutting, CNC, edge banding, installation, finishing, delivery), together with materials and stock, deadlines by stage, reports and attachments. Everything is in one place and available to the whole team.

Does it need installation or special hardware?

No. It runs in the browser on a computer, tablet and phone. You can try the live prototype right away, with no installation and no preparation.

How quickly is it introduced and what does the collaboration look like?

We start with an analysis of your process and a clickable prototype, then build in phases. You typically see the first usable result in 3 to 5 weeks. The solution is custom, adapted to your way of working, and after launch we stay with you for training and improvements.

Who is it for?

For custom furniture manufacturers (kitchens, wardrobes, interiors) and production plants in Bosnia and Herzegovina and Croatia that want a clear overview of production instead of a notebook and spreadsheets.
