Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14.08.2026.

This privacy policy explains how Datamaks d.o.o. ("Datamaks", "we") collects, uses, stores and protects the personal data of visitors to the website datamaks.net and users of our services. We respect your privacy and process data in accordance with the Law on Personal Data Protection of Bosnia and Herzegovina and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. What data we collect

  • Contact form data: name, email address, phone number, company name and the content of the message you send us.
  • Prototype generator (mockup) data: email, phone and a description of your business and the problem you enter so that we can build a demo prototype.
  • Technical data and analytics: anonymized visit data (device type, approximate location at city or country level, pages you visit, source of the visit) through a web analytics tool.
  • Cookies: we use necessary cookies and, with your consent, analytics cookies (see section 4).

We do not knowingly collect special categories of data (for example health or biometric data) or data of children.

2. Purposes and legal basis of processing

  • Responding to inquiries and providing the service (prototype creation, quotes, communication). Legal basis: taking steps at your request and our legitimate interest.
  • Improving the website and measuring effectiveness. Legal basis: your consent to analytics cookies.
  • Advertising and reach (for example measuring campaigns on social networks). Legal basis: your consent, where applicable.
  • Compliance with legal obligations. Legal basis: legal obligation.

3. How long we keep data

We keep data from inquiries and the prototype generator for as long as is necessary for communication and possible collaboration, and for no more than 24 months from the last contact, unless the law requires otherwise. We keep analytics data in aggregated form in accordance with the settings of the analytics tool.

4. Cookies and analytics

The website uses cookies necessary for the operation of the site and, with your consent, analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) to understand how the site is used. You give consent through the cookie notice and can withdraw it at any time by deleting cookies in your browser. IP addresses are processed in anonymized form in analytics.

5. Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell your data. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run the website and communicate, and only to the minimum extent necessary:

  • Supabase: database hosting and processing of submitted forms.
  • Google Analytics: web analytics (with your consent).
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram): measuring and displaying ads, when campaigns are active.
  • Email service providers: delivery of notifications about your inquiries.

Some of these providers may process data outside Bosnia and Herzegovina. In that case, appropriate safeguards provided by regulations apply.

6. Your rights

In accordance with applicable data protection regulations, you have the right to:

  • request access to the data we process about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate data or completion of incomplete data;
  • request erasure of data;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with the competent Personal Data Protection Agency.

To exercise any right, write to us at info@datamaks.net. We will respond within a reasonable time, at the latest within the period prescribed by law.

7. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access, loss or misuse (encrypted data transfer, access control, regular system maintenance).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version takes effect when published on this page, with a changed date at the top. We recommend that you review it periodically.

9. Contact

For any questions about privacy and the processing of your data, contact us at info@datamaks.net or at the address Datamaks d.o.o., Veljka Mlađenovića bb, 78000 Banja Luka, Bosnia and Herzegovina.
