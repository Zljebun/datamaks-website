Frequently Asked Questions

How much does business digitalization cost?

The cost depends on the project scope. We offer a free initial consultation where we analyze your needs and provide an estimate. We work in phases, so you don't have to invest everything at once.

What is business digitalization?

Business digitalization is the process of replacing manual, paper-based processes with automated software solutions. This includes automating daily tasks, centralizing data, and creating reports for better business decisions.

How long does software implementation take?

You get a prototype built on your own examples quickly, usually the same day. You typically see the first usable result in 3 to 5 weeks, while more complex systems take 2 to 3 months. We work in phases so you see results as early as possible.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes, we specialize in small and medium enterprises. Our solutions are tailored to your needs and budget, you don't pay for features you don't need.
